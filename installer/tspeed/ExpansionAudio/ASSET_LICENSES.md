# Audio asset licenses

The checked-in source recordings are CC0 assets obtained from OpenGameArt:

- `source/car_sound_effects_pack.zip`: Car Sound Effects Pack, CC0. Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/car-sound-effects-pack-low-quality
- `source/gatve_varniu.ogg`: High traffic road sounds, CC0. Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/high-traffic-road-sounds
- `source/sfx_100_v2.zip`: 100 CC0 SFX #2, CC0. Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2

`ambience/street_01.ogg` through `street_15.ogg` are derived mixes of `base_road_cc0.ogg` with documented filter variation. They are not recordings made in the named countries; they are licensed base ambience variants used as the first integration pass. Replace them with country-specific field recordings only after verifying each recording's license and provenance.
