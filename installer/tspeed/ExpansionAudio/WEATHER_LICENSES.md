# Weather audio licenses

The weather layer uses derived OGG loops made from the CC0 source recordings already listed in `ASSET_LICENSES.md`:

- `sfx100v2_air_01.ogg`, `sfx100v2_air_02.ogg`, `sfx100v2_air_03.ogg`, `sfx100v2_loop_water_01.ogg`, and `sfx100v2_thunder_01.ogg` come from the CC0 `100 CC0 SFX #2` pack: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2
- Road ambience variants come from the CC0 high-traffic road recording: https://opengameart.org/content/high-traffic-road-sounds

The files in `weather/` are filtered/looped derivatives. Names such as desert, mountain and farm identify the simulation profile and do not claim that the recording was made in those locations.
